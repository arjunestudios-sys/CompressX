1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Docholder ("we", "our", or "us"). Docholder is an intelligent document management, universal transformation, high-efficiency compression, and multimedia utility platform.

We respect your fundamental privacy rights. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we process, the technical measures employed to safeguard your data, and how you can exercise full control over your files and accounts.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account Credentials (Optional): If you choose to create a Docholder account for cloud synchronization, we collect your email address, username, and password. Passwords are immediately salted and hashed with bcrypt before storage. Core tools are fully functional without creating an account.
  • User Uploaded Files: Files and documents (PDFs, Microsoft Word DOCX, Excel XLSX, Images, Videos, Audio) you select for compression, conversion, watermarking, encryption, or redaction.

B. Information Processed Automatically

  • Diagnostic & Network Information: Basic IP addresses and request headers for routing, DDOS mitigation, and rate limiting.
  • Local Transformation History: Your recent activity logs are stored locally on your device in your browser's IndexedDB / LocalStorage, and can be cleared instantly in the app Settings.

C. Information We Never Collect

  • We do not collect contacts, device identifiers (IMEI / MAC), phone numbers, or GPS location data.
  • We do not sell, license, or monetize your documents.

3. File Processing & Automatic 1-Hour Purge Lifecycle

Docholder follows a strict ephemeral lifecycle for all processed assets:

  • Local-First Processing: Lightweight tasks like image resizing, basic PDF reordering, and canvas drawing execute 100% inside your client browser / WebView memory.
  • Secure Cloud Pipelines: When you execute demanding operations (such as high-ratio video compression via FFmpeg or complex document conversions), files are transferred via TLS 1.3 to our secure backend worker nodes.
  • Automatic 1-Hour Purge: All temporary upload files and output artifacts in server scratch directories are deleted permanently by background cron tasks every 60 minutes.
  • Encrypted Private Vaults (Optional): If you deliberately choose to store documents in your personal cloud vault, they are kept encrypted with JWT-authenticated access controls until you choose to delete them.

4. Third-Party Integrations & AI Processing

Docholder includes modern AI utilities (such as document summarization and AI text assistants) powered by Google Gemini API. When you activate these optional tools:

  • Only the specific text snippet you request to analyze is transmitted securely via HTTPS.
  • Your data is processed in real time and is not used to train global public AI models.
  • No third-party behavioral advertising SDKs (e.g., AdMob or Meta) are embedded within the app.

5. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We maintain strict security safeguards across all layers of Docholder:

  • Transport Encryption: All communications between the app and backend APIs use HTTPS (TLS 1.3) with HSTS enforcement.
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: User passwords are encrypted with bcrypt utilizing unique per-user cryptographic salts.
  • Document Redaction Tools: Built-in privacy scanning tools allow you to detect and redact sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) before exporting or sharing files.

6. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We provide universal privacy controls to all users worldwide:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can download and export your vault files at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ('Right to be Forgotten'): You can delete your account, wipe all uploaded documents, and clear transformation history directly in Settings > Storage & Data Management.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can operate Docholder completely offline for local-compatible tools.

7. Children's Privacy

Docholder is not targeted at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information, please contact us for immediate deletion.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We recommend checking this page periodically for updates.
